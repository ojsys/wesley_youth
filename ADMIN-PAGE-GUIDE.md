# Website Manager — how to update the website

Everything on the website is edited from one place: the **Website Manager**.

Go to **`yourdomain.com/admin`** and sign in with your password.
(There's also a small *Site admin* link at the very bottom of the website.)

Bookmark that address — it's the only page you need.

---

## How editing works

1. Open **Pages & sections**.
2. Click the part of the page you want to change. Change it. Nothing is live yet.
3. Press **Publish changes** (top right) when you're happy.

Until you press Publish, visitors still see the old version — so you can take your
time, and a half-finished headline never appears on the real site.

The button at the top tells you where you stand:

| What it says | What it means |
|---|---|
| **All changes published** | The website matches what you see here. |
| **● Unpublished changes** | You've edited something. Press Publish. |

Tips:
- Press **Ctrl+S** (Windows) or **Cmd+S** (Mac) to publish.
- If you close the tab with unpublished changes, your work is kept. Next time you sign
  in it offers to bring it back.

## The page builder

**Pages & sections** has three parts:

```
┌──────────┬─────────────────────────┬──────────────┐
│  PAGES   │      THE REAL PAGE      │   SETTINGS   │
│          │                         │              │
│ • Home   │   Click anything here   │  Everything  │
│ • About  │   to select it          │  about what  │
│ • Events │                         │  you clicked │
│          │                         │              │
│ + New    │                         │              │
└──────────┴─────────────────────────┴──────────────┘
```

- **Left** — your pages. Click one to open it.
- **Middle** — the actual website. Not a picture of it: the real thing. Click any
  heading, photo, button or block to select it.
- **Right** — the settings for whatever you selected. Change them and the middle
  updates as you type.

When nothing is selected, the right-hand panel shows the **page itself**: a list of its
sections, its name and address, and its search-engine wording.

At the top of the right-hand panel is a trail like
**Home › Impact Areas › Row › Column › Heading**. Click any step to go back out to it.

### The buttons on each part

Hover over anything in the middle and a small dark toolbar appears on it:

| Button | What it does |
|---|---|
| ↑ ↓ | Move it up or down |
| 👁 | Hide a section from visitors (without deleting it) |
| ⧉ | Make a copy |
| 🗑 | Delete it |
| ＋ | Add a section after this one, or add an element into a column |

A hidden section still shows in the builder, marked *Hidden*, so you don't lose track
of it. Visitors don't see it at all.

### Phone and tablet

The three buttons at the top of the middle panel (🖥 📱) show you how the page looks on
a narrower screen. They only change the preview, never the site.

## Sections

A **section** is one horizontal band of the page. Press **Add a section** (at the bottom
of the page, or the ＋ on any section's toolbar) and pick one.

**Headers**
- **Hero** — the big opening of the home page.
- **Page header** — a title band for an inner page, with a trail back to Home.
- **Slideshow** — rotating photos with words and buttons over them.

**Church** — the sections the site was originally built from. They look exactly as they
always have, but now they can be moved, copied, hidden, or used on any page:
- **Vision & Mission**
- **Impact Areas** — exactly 4 arranges them around the emblem; any other number uses a
  plain grid.
- **Gatherings** — the event cards.
- **Photo Gallery**
- **Contact & form**

**Build your own** — start from a blank section, or from a ready-made arrangement:
heading and text, text beside a photo, three features, a call-to-action banner,
questions and answers, a numbers strip, people, a quote, a video, or a map beside a
form. Each one is just a normal section afterwards, so you can change anything in it.

### Every section's background and spacing

Select a section and open **Background & spacing** on the right:

- **Background** — cream, paper, sand, dark, gold, a colour you choose, or a photo.
- **How wide the content runs** — normal, narrow (good for reading), medium, or edge to edge.
- **Space above / below**.
- **Link name (anchor)** — type `vision` here and a menu link to `#vision` jumps to
  this section.

## Rows, columns and elements

A blank section is built from **rows**. Each row is split into **columns**. Each column
holds **elements**. That is all there is to it.

- **Add a row** — at the bottom of the section, or from its settings. You pick how many
  columns: one, two equal, narrow + wide, three, four, and so on.
- **Add an element** — the ＋ on a column's toolbar, or **Add an element here** in the
  column's settings.

Changing a row's layout never loses anything. Going from three columns to one puts all
the elements together in the single column; going the other way adds empty columns for
you to fill.

### The elements you can add

**Basic** — Heading · Text · Image · Buttons · Icon · Icon + text
**Layout** — Blank space · Divider line
**Media** — Video (YouTube, Vimeo or an .mp4 link) · Photo grid · Map
**Content** — Cards · Tick list · Quote · Numbers · Questions (accordion) ·
Social links · Logo/emblem · Contact form · Address & phone
**Advanced** — Custom code, for embed codes from other services

The **Text** element has a small toolbar: bold, italic, a heading, a link, bullet and
numbered lists, a quote, and a button to strip formatting.

## Pages

The site starts with three: **Home**, **About** (`/about`) and **Contact**
(`/contact`). About and Contact come already written — a story, what to expect on a
first visit, questions people ask, and on Contact a message form, a map, how to get
here and the service times. They are ordinary pages: change the wording, swap the
photo, add sections, or delete them entirely.

**New page** (bottom left) asks for a name and creates the page with a title header
already on it.

Select nothing, and the right-hand panel shows **Page settings**:

- **Page name** — shown in the footer and the browser tab.
- **Address** — the page lives at `/` plus this, e.g. `/about-us`. Letters, numbers and
  dashes only.
- **List this page in the footer** — on by default.
- **Make this the home page** — the old home page keeps its content and gets an address
  of its own.
- **Delete page**.
- **Search engines** — the browser tab title and the sentence Google shows under the
  link. Leave them empty and the page name is used.

To put a page in the **top menu**, go to **Menu** and add a link — the dropdown lists
every page, so you don't have to type an address.

> New pages only work on the live site once you **Publish**.

## What's in the menu

**Overview**
- **Dashboard** — a summary of the site, your pages, and the latest messages.
- **Messages** — everything sent through the contact form (see below).

**The website**
- **Pages & sections** — the page builder.

**Site-wide** — things that are the same on every page:
- **Identity & Logo** — church name, subtitle, logo, browser tab title and tab icon
  (favicon), a height slider for the logo in the top bar (28–62px, since the bar itself
  is a fixed 70px), a width slider that lets a wide logo run broader across the brand
  area, a size slider for the rest of the page, and switches to show or hide the name
  and subtitle beside the logo.
- **Menu** — the links across the top of every page.
- **Contact & Socials** — the address, email, phone and social links. Typed once here
  and used everywhere: the contact section, the address block and the footer all read
  from this.
- **Footer**

**Tools**
- **Media Library** — every photo you've uploaded.
- **History** — put back an earlier version, or download a backup.
- **Settings** — where contact form messages are sent, and your password.

## Photos

Anywhere the editor asks for a photo, press **Choose photo**. You can either drag a
photo in from your computer or phone, or pick one you've already uploaded.

Uploaded photos are stored on the website itself and are available everywhere from
then on. JPG, PNG, GIF and WEBP up to 8 MB each.

> **Tip:** resize very large photos to about 1600px wide before uploading — the pages
> load noticeably faster.

### Describing your photos

In **Media Library** each photo has two boxes under it:

- **Alt text** — a short description of what's in the picture, like *"Students sharing
  a meal together"*. Visitors who use a screen reader hear this instead of seeing the
  photo, and it shows if the image ever fails to load. It also helps Google.
- **Caption** — the words you'd normally put with the photo.

Both save the moment you click away — they describe the photo itself, so they don't
wait for **Publish changes**. Once filled in, they're offered automatically as the
title and caption whenever you add that photo somewhere.

## Lists inside a section

Some sections and elements hold a list — the gathering cards, the gallery photos, the
impact areas, the slides in a slideshow, the questions in an accordion. Each entry has
buttons in its header:

- **↑ ↓** move it up or down — this is the order visitors see.
- **⧉** make a copy of it.
- **🗑** delete it.

The **Add** button at the bottom of the list creates a new one.

## Social links

Facebook, Instagram and YouTube are set up out of the box. To add another
(TikTok, X, WhatsApp, GroupMe, Spotify…), pick it from the dropdown at the bottom of
**Contact & Socials** and press **Add this social**, then paste your page's link.

To remove one, press its 🗑 button.

## Messages from the contact form

When someone fills in the form on the website:

1. It is **emailed** to the address in **Settings → Contact form**
   (currently `odanladi@icfirstchurch.org`). Just hit Reply to answer them.
2. A copy is **always kept** under **Messages**, even if the email fails — so nothing
   is ever lost.

Unread messages show a red count next to **Messages** in the menu. Click a message to
read it, then use **Reply by email**, **Mark unread** or **Delete**.

To change where messages go, edit the address in **Settings** and press
**Save address**. Use **Send a test email** to check it arrives — remember to look in
the spam folder the first time.

This works wherever you put a **Contact form** element, on any page.

## If something goes wrong

**History** keeps a copy of the website from every time you publish. Find the version
from before the mistake and press **Restore this version** — it goes live immediately,
and your current version is saved first so you can undo the undo.

### "All our content has disappeared!"

It almost certainly has not. Your words and photos live in the database, not in the
website's files, so a bad upload cannot delete them.

1. **If the website shows a message naming a missing file** — upload that file to the
   server next to `index.html` and reload. That is the whole fix.
2. **If the website looks like a stranger's site** (sample text you never wrote), the
   editor was showing the built-in starter content and it got published over yours.
   Go to **History**, find the version from just before, and press
   **Restore this version**.
3. **If the page is completely blank**, the site is missing `blocks.js`, `render.js` or
   `theme.css` — three files added when the page builder arrived. Upload them and
   reload.

Whatever happened, don't press **Publish changes** until the site looks right again —
publishing is what would replace the good copy.

You can also press **Download a backup** before any big change, and
**Load a backup file** to bring it back.

## Your password

Change it under **Settings → Password**: type the current one, then a new one of at
least 8 characters.

If you're locked out, whoever set the site up can reset it by editing `config.php` —
see **`DEPLOY-HPANEL.md`**.
